## DirSizes 0.1.0

The first release.

- Scans folders and whole disks in parallel with `getattrlistbulk(2)`.
- Sunburst chart with zoom, hover details, and free and hidden space for disk scans.
- Collector for moving found files to the Trash or deleting them.
- `dirsizes` command-line scanner and benchmark, linked by the Homebrew cask.
